Frequently asked

What ages is MathPet for?

Designed for 4–7-year-olds. Younger kids can play with help; older kids may move through the levels quickly.

Does my child need to read?

No. The interface is visual. We use icons, numbers, and short tap-to-hear voice prompts (when enabled).

Do you collect data on my child?

We collect the minimum needed to run the app: account email (provided by the parent), in-app progress, and crash reports. We do not sell data, do not run ads, do not use behavioral profiling. Full details in our Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Through your App Store or Google Play account settings. We don't have access to cancel for you because we never see your billing details — Apple and Google handle payments.

Is there a refund policy?

Refunds are handled by Apple and Google directly. We can't issue them on our side. The 7-day free trial is meant to let you decide before paying anything.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The math engine and pet progression run fully offline. Sync happens when you're back online.

How do I contact support?

Email support@mathpet.app. We try to reply within 48 hours.
